# Rubric for cold-visitor and judge scoring

Scale 1 to 10 per line. 10 means "I would act on this now without talking to anyone". When torn between two scores, take the lower. Every score carries one exact quote from the page that drove it.

| Line | Question the visitor answers |
|---|---|
| L1 Clear in 10 seconds | From the first screen alone: what is this, who is it for, is it for me? |
| L2 I recognise my pain | Does the page describe my situation in words I would use? |
| L3 I can picture what I walk away with | Can I see my week or month after buying it? |
| L4 What, how long, how much, before I choose | Do I know what I get, over how long, for how much, before the page asks me to pick an option? |
| L5 My effort small, their work visible | Is my part clear and small, and can I see what the seller does? Does the effort claim survive my own arithmetic? |
| L6 I believe it | Does the proof convince someone like me? |
| L7 Risk handled | Is what I risk handled, near the button? |
| L8 Next step doable alone | Do I know what happens when I click, and can I do it without a call? |
| L9 Forwardable | Could I forward this to the person who signs, and would it hold up? Score only when a company buys and someone else signs. |
| L10 Calm and premium | Does it feel calm, premium and distinctive, or generic? |

Plus:
- **Overall** 1 to 10.
- **Act now without a call:** yes / maybe, pending X / no. X is what would have to change.
- **Chance I would submit the form today:** 0 to 100 percent.
- **Up to three blockers**, each with the section.

## Comprehension questions (quick mode and stop rule)
1. What is this?
2. Who is it for, and is it for me?
3. What do I get?
4. Over how long?
5. For how much?
6. What happens when I act?
A visitor who gets any of these wrong, or cannot answer, is a comprehension gap. Comprehension gaps are never capped by the seller's decisions.

## Reading scores
- Differences under 2 points between runs or versions are noise.
- The pattern across seats matters more than any single number. Belief (L6) is usually capped by the proof that exists, not by craft; report it as a cap and move on.
- The submit chance is a simulated estimate; use it to compare versions, never as a forecast.
