# Persona packs

A persona pack is a short file a reviewer subagent reads to become one visitor. Quality of the panel depends on two things: the person is like the real ICP, and the person knows nothing about the seller. Prospect-modelled packs that "know the seller" or carry the offer's history score comprehension too high; that is the failure this skill exists to prevent.

## The seats

| Seat | How many | Who |
|---|---|---|
| Cold visitor | 3 | People like the ICP in GOAL.md. Vary segment, company size, channel (LinkedIn DM, cold email, forwarded WhatsApp) and device (at least two on phones). |
| Skeptic | 1 | Someone in the ICP who bought the alternative and was burnt (fired the agency, the tool went unused, the consultant overpromised). Fair: says when something answers the objection. |
| Forwarded role | 0 to 3 | Only where a company buys and someone else signs: budget owner, IT or security, procurement. Each gets the link forwarded by a colleague with one line of context. |
| Held-back judge | 4 | For ab mode only: three like the ICP plus one skeptic, different people from the loop panel. They never see a page during the loop, so they are fresh for the final comparison. |

## Template (keep each pack under 20 lines)

```
# <Seat> <n>
## Who you are
<Role, company type and size, city, age. What they sell or do, deal sizes or rates. Their current habit around the problem.>
## How you got here
<Channel and who sent it, one line of what the message said, device and moment. The link preview is the first thing you see.>
## What you want
<The outcome in their own words.>
## What you are wary of
<Three or four specific worries, including what burnt them or people like them.>
## How you read
<Device, speed, what they check first (price, proof, effort), when they leave.>
```

## Rules
- No knowledge of the seller, the offer, its history, earlier versions or the brief.
- Write in plain words; give each person a concrete business with numbers so their judgement has something to stand on.
- For buyers in a specific market (for example German-speaking Europe) say so: language, currency and legal expectations change verdicts.
- Reuse the same packs across rounds so score changes reflect the page, not the panel.
- Store packs in `review/personas/` (cold-1.md ... skeptic.md, fwd-budget.md ..., judge-1.md ... judge-skeptic.md).
