# Style

Applies to every file, row and page in a run.

**Plain:**
- Lead with the answer. Say what happened, who did it, and when.
- Short sentences, ordinary words. No hype.
- Numbers with dates and units. Ranges when a range is the honest answer.

**Never:**
- The em-dash character (U+2014). Use commas, colons, parentheses or a new sentence.
- "It's not X, it's Y", "not just X but Y", "the hard part was never X", or similar. State the positive claim directly.
- "Game-changer", "revolutionary", "unlock", "seamless", "robust", "leverage" as a verb.
- Invented numbers, sources or quotes.
- A typed number in page markup or prose that could be computed: use a `{{stat:id}}` token.

**Honesty:**
- A vendor's number is "vendor-stated".
- An estimate is "estimate" with its assumptions and range.
- "May" stays "may". One case stays one case.
- Say what you could not establish.

**People:** public roles only. Describe what someone did, and skip judgements of character. Do not publish personal emails, home addresses or private details. Use they/them unless a person's pronouns are stated.

**Quotes:** under 25 words, attributed. Summarise anything longer.
