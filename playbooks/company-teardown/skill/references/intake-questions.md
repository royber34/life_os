# Intake questions

Ask these in one message, each with its default. Skip anything the user's notes already answer. "Go with your defaults" is a valid answer to all of them.

1. **Which company, and what is its main domain?** If several share the name, confirm which one.

2. **Who will act on this, and what do they need to decide?** Examples: running the brand, advising it, copying its playbook, entering its market, partnering, investing, competing. This sets the opening Moves tab. Default: the person running the business or unit in scope.

3. **Whole company, or one business unit, brand, product or market?** Example: one brand's US business. Default: the whole company worldwide, unless it is large, in which case the probe will ask you to narrow it. With a market scope, the research still covers the parent and global history where they explain that market, and it says when a figure is global and not market-specific.

4. **How deep?** Default: a dated event log with a source on every row, plus written sections. The first full run on treg produced over a thousand rows, and a company with less public material will produce far fewer.

5. **Output?** Default: the interactive page, opening on the overview (with the SWOT), then so what (what to double down on, quick wins, budget shifts, bigger bets), timeline, go-to-market, AI, competition and deep dive, plus a one-page decision memo. Status and so what are kept apart.

6. **Which paid data may it use, and how much?** Default: treg if connected, up to USD 5 for the run, nothing above USD 0.30 per call. The run reports what it could not get at that level.

7. **Is there a specific question or adjacent market to apply it to?** Example: "what would this model look like in my industry?" Default: none. A named lens adds a separate section, and the research stays about the company itself.

8. **Who else will read it?** Default: only you. This sets tone and what the page may contain.

9. **What do you already know or believe about it?** Default: nothing stated. Anything given becomes a hypothesis to test, and the agents do not see it as fact.

10. **Time limit and stop rule?** Default: about 4 hours of active work, and at the limit it stops and reports what is done.

Also state these, with no question needed:
- No one is contacted during the run. It ends with who to call and what to ask.
- The checks add about half again to the research work.
- A run folder can hold paid data and personal names, so it stays private unless you say otherwise.
