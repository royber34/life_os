# North Star

> Last confirmed: YYYY-MM-DD

Every weekly rock has to tie back to a line in this file. Keep it short and honest; update it when something stops being true and change the date above.

## 1. How I want my weeks to feel
*One line, in your own words.*

## 2. Goals
*Life and work, 3 to 6 lines. Include your "enough" line if you have one (the income or output that is enough, and how much of your time you want it to take). Anything above that line is never a reason for a rock.*

## 3. Roles
*The 3 to 6 roles you play (for example: founder, consultant, parent, partner, athlete). Rocks are spread across them on purpose.*

## 4. Values
*3 to 5 words or short lines. Used as a tie-breaker.*

## 5. This quarter's focus
*The one win that would make this quarter a success, in your own words.*

## 6. Not the goal
*Things you deliberately do not chase right now. Helps the planner fill the "Not this week" list.*

## 7. Setup (optional)
- Notes folder: *path to a folder of notes or journals the planner should read, or leave blank*
- Slack channel to post the plan to: *your own channel, or leave blank to save to a file only*
- Typical focus hours per week: *used when your calendar is too sparse to estimate*
- Sources to skip: *for example "WhatsApp"*
